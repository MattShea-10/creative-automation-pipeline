Optimize the prompt for backdrops (recommended)
Adds "sharp focus, crisp detail, no faces, no text, space for overlaid text" to your prompt. Models are worst at faces, hands and lettering -- exactly what a backdrop doesn't need -- so steering away from them removes most of what reads as distortion. It asks for sharpness, not softness: use the band and glow controls to keep text legible over a crisp backdrop. The full prompt used is shown on the results page.

Update layers across all template sizes -- fill any of these in and, on every template-covered size whose PSD has a matching named layer (header, description, logo, cta, product, background), the new content is composited into that layer's exact position for that size -- a different position/scale per size, since each template has its own layout. These are PSD updates only -- they don't affect the general hero-image tool below.
