Pristine copies of the sample briefs the app ships with.

The five cards the form opens with come from these two files:
  Winter Glow 2026    -- HydroBoost Sports Drink, FreshGlow Body Wash, PureShine Shampoo
  Summer Refresh 2026 -- HydroBoost Sports Drink, FreshGlow Body Wash

"Remove" on a card deletes that product from the live brief file, so it
stays gone across restarts. If you want the original set back, copy the
file you want from here over the one in briefs/ and reload the page.

Nothing in the app writes to this folder.
